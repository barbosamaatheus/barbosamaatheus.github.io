A hobby project: a party-game hub to play on the TV with friends, using smartphones as controllers. The TV shows the shared table while each phone becomes the player's hand, and the whole hub can be navigated from the couch using the phone as a remote control.

<!--more-->

The library currently has more than 30 games, such as *Gatos Explosivos*, a card game inspired by Exploding Kittens for 2 to 9 players.
